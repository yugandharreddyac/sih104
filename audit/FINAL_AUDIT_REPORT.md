## 3. Problem Statement & Solution Overview

### The Threat:
Adversaries use modern generative AI voice cloning tools to replicate customer or executive voices from short audio snippets. Attackers deploy these voice clones during live phone calls to social engineer bank representatives, bypass legacy voice biometrics, and authorize fraudulent wire transfers or steal one-time passwords (OTPs).

### The VOXSHIELD Solution:
VOXSHIELD provides a real-time, multi-layered defense architecture:
1. **Acoustic Deepfake Detection:** Detects vocoder spectral artifacts and phase anomalies via `MiniAcousticCNN` and Wav2Vec2 ONNX models.
2. **Speaker Biometric Verification:** Extracts 192-D `ECAPA-TDNN` vector embeddings and computes cosine similarity against enrolled customer voiceprints.
3. **Multilingual Conversational Intelligence:** Runs streaming ASR (Faster-Whisper), sanitizes financial credentials via an in-memory Privacy Firewall, and detects social engineering tactics (urgency, authority impersonation, coercion).
4. **10-D Multi-Modal Risk Fusion:** Aggregates all acoustic, biometric, conversational, and transactional signals into an explainable 10-dimensional risk tensor.
5. **Deterministic Policy Engine & SOC Governance:** Enforces rule-based interventions (Allow, Warn Agent, Require Step-Up Auth, Terminate Call) and dispatches HMAC-signed webhooks to core banking systems.

## 15. Known Limitations & Honest Engineering Disclosures

1. **Commercial Proprietary Cloners:** Emerging closed-source voice engines (e.g. ElevenLabs v3) require continuous model updates. VOXSHIELD mitigates this via its conversational and biometric fusion layers.
2. **Severely Degraded 8 kHz Cellular Lines:** Severe cellular packet loss can reduce spectral resolution; mitigated by dual-mode channel-aware thresholding ($\theta=0.525$ for telephony).
3. **Indic Language ASR:** High ambient background noise can affect ASR accuracy; mitigated by confidence-damped conversational weighting.
