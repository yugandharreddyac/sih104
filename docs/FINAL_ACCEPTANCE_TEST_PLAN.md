# SIH104 / VOXSHIELD — Final Acceptance Test Plan

This document specifies the comprehensive acceptance test suite that validates end-to-end integration, deterministic safety policies, threat fusion accuracy, and perimeter security across VOXSHIELD prior to production release.

---

## Safety Invariant Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Cardinal Safety Rule**: A low acoustic deepfake score ($<0.15$) must **NOT** cause a human voice requesting OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords, or attempting social engineering/unverified financial transactions, to be evaluated as `SAFE` / `ALLOW`. Threat dimensions operate independently, and conversational risk overrides acoustic legitimacy.
