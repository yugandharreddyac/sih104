# VOXSHIELD Research Datasets & Benchmarks

## 1. Dataset Index

### ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access (LA)
- **Task**: Synthetic speech (TTS) and Voice Conversion (VC) anti-spoofing.
- **Language**: English (VCTK base speakers).
- **Sampling Rate**: 16 kHz.
- **License**: Open Academic Research (ASVspoof Consortium).
- **Usage**: Baseline feature calibration for LFCC higher-order variance and neural vocoder phase artifacts.

### ASVspoof 2019 Physical Access (PA)
- **Task**: Acoustic and physical replay attack detection.
- **Sampling Rate**: 16 kHz.
- **License**: Open Academic Research.
- **Usage**: Benchmark for high-frequency spectral roll-off and double reverberation decay.

### VoxCeleb 1 & 2
- **Task**: Speaker identification and biometric verification.
- **Language**: Multilingual.
- **Sampling Rate**: 16 kHz.
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
- **Usage**: Projection basis for 128-dimensional acoustic x-vector embedding representations.
