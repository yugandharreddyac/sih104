# VOXSHIELD: Phase 3 Acoustic Deepfake & Speaker Biometric Intelligence

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 3 establishes the **Acoustic Intelligence & Biometric layer** within VOXSHIELD. It evaluates incoming audio across four independent non-verbal acoustic domains:

1. **Synthetic / Deepfake Voice Detection** (AASIST/RawNet2-aligned spectral & vocoder artifact analysis).
2. **Speaker Biometric Verification & Enrollment** (128-dimensional x-vector embedding extraction and cosine similarity matching).
3. **Replay Attack Detection** (High-frequency roll-off, room impulse decay, and transducer non-linear distortion analysis).
4. **Audio Injection & Manipulation Indicators** (Discontinuity jumps, splicing boundaries, and synthetic packet repetition).

## 2. Deepfake Detection Architecture

```
Incoming Audio (PCM 16kHz)
  ↓
AudioPreprocessor (Pre-emphasis filter alpha=0.97, DC centering, Hamming framing)
  ↓
AcousticFeatureExtractor (Log-Mel Filterbanks, 20-bin LFCC, Spectral Flatness, Phase Distortion)
  ↓
DeepfakeAcousticModel (ASVspoof 2019/2021 LA aligned acoustic neural classifier)
  ↓
DeepfakeCalibrator (Quality degradation penalty, decision thresholding, explainability compilation)
  ↓
DeepfakeAnalysisResult (AUTHENTIC | SUSPICIOUS | INCONCLUSIVE | INSUFFICIENT_AUDIO)
```

### Key Mathematical Formulations
- **Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (LFCC)**: Computed with linearly spaced filterbanks to capture high-frequency spectral artifacts generated by neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, MelGAN, WaveNet).
- **Spectral Flatness (Wiener Entropy)**:
  $$\text{Flatness} = \frac{\exp\left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N \ln S[k]\right)}{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{k=1}^N S[k]}$$

## 3. Speaker Biometric Verification & Anti-Spoof Enrollment

### Anti-Spoof Gated Enrollment
Speaker profiles are created strictly through multi-utterance verification:
```
Utterance 1 + Utterance 2
  ↓
Quality Pre-Screening (Rejects clipping > 10% or SNR < 6dB)
  ↓
Anti-Spoof Screening (Rejects synthetic or TTS input)
  ↓
x-Vector Embedding Extraction (128-dim L2-normalized representation)
  ↓
Centroid Normalization & Biometric Profile Storage (Zero raw audio stored)
```

### Verification Decision Threshold
- $\text{Cosine Similarity} \ge 0.70 \implies \text{MATCH}$
- $\text{Cosine Similarity} < 0.70 \implies \text{MISMATCH}$

## 4. Replay Attack Detection

Replay detection identifies cues of physical loudspeaker playback:
- **High-Frequency Spectral Cutoff**: Transducers severely attenuate frequencies above 4.5 kHz.
- **Double Reverberation Decay Time**: Autocorrelation envelope decay measuring secondary room impulse response ($>120$ ms).
- **Transducer Non-Linear Harmonic Distortion**: Discrete second-derivative variance of the signal.

---

## 5. Temporal Aggregation & Stability

A rolling 1.0 to 3.0 second temporal buffer aggregates multi-chunk predictions:
- **Warm-Up Period**: Minimum 600 ms of active speech required before stabilizing confidence.
- **Median Spoof Aggregation**: Suppresses single-chunk spurious spikes.
- **Overall Assessment Formulation**: Emits `AUTHENTICITY_SUPPORTED`, `SUSPICIOUS`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `INSUFFICIENT_AUDIO`.
